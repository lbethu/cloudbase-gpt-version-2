# Reviewer Notes

## What is mock

The 22-document COM, SOP, Sales, PM/PL, Field, Technical, and Training corpus is fictional. It demonstrates realistic structure and employee workflows but must not be treated as approved company guidance.

## What is being tested

- Source-group and team navigation
- Rich document metadata
- Question retrieval and confidence
- Most-relevant and supporting sources
- Related documents and example questions
- Source inspection and replacement notes
- Upload and review workflow
- Coverage and integration planning

## Real data needed later

- Authoritative files and pages
- Source-system identifiers
- Document owners and reviewers
- Effective and review dates
- Audience and permission groups
- Confidentiality classifications
- Superseded-version relationships
- Approved example questions and evaluation answers

## Approval decisions

- Final source-group taxonomy
- Initial pilot audience
- First real collection to connect
- Access-control and hosting requirements
- Content-owner review workflow
- Retrieval-quality and citation thresholds
- Support, feedback, and retirement ownership

## Recommended demonstration

Open Team Dashboards, select each audience, ask representative questions, inspect a source's metadata and related documents, review the coverage matrix, and finish in Reviewer Mode.
