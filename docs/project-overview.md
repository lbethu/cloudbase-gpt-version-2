# Project Overview

## Product vision

CloudBase AI is an internal company knowledge, AI guidance, and future workflow automation foundation. Its purpose is to shorten the distance between an employee's question and the governed operating guidance needed to act.

## Problem

Important knowledge commonly spans SOPs, project folders, delivery checklists, internal sites, sales materials, role guides, templates, and training resources. Employees can lose time locating the current source, interpreting its relevance, or identifying the correct owner.

## Solution

The prototype combines a source-backed question experience with category navigation, a governed document library, upload staging, source inspection, and an explicit real-data integration pathway. It makes the source visible rather than presenting an untraceable chatbot answer.

## Version 1 prototype scope

The approval-facing prototype validates the experience using 30 fictional company-style sources and 109 chunks across 10 source groups, seven team dashboards, and 10 guidance modules. It includes no external AI API, backend storage, authentication, production connector, or supplied company document. The local retrieval implementation is intentionally transparent and replaceable.

## Product principles

- Sources remain visible and reviewable.
- The system should abstain when evidence is weak.
- Metadata and ownership are first-class product concepts.
- High-impact guidance should be verified at the source.
- Real content should enter through a governed review workflow.
- The user experience should survive a future retrieval-engine replacement.
