# CloudBase AI Executive Product Walkthrough

## Presentation objective

Explain the CloudBase AI company-wide knowledge, guidance, and future automation concept in five to seven minutes, then leave the reviewer with a small set of concrete pilot decisions.

The walkthrough is available at `/presentation`.

## Recommended demo flow

### 0:00–0:40 — Opening

- Introduce the SOP dashboard as the first proof point and CloudBase AI as the broader company-wide foundation.
- State immediately that the approval-facing corpus is realistic mock company-style data.
- Emphasize the three values: centralized knowledge, source-backed answers, and a replaceable real-data-ready architecture.

### 0:40–1:20 — Problem and solution

- Describe the general challenge of knowledge becoming distributed across sites, folders, playbooks, templates, and team notes.
- Do not claim this is the company's confirmed current state.
- Show the employee-question-to-source-backed-answer flow.

### 1:20–2:25 — Architecture and mock strategy

- Walk through the seven source groups.
- Explain that every document follows one metadata, chunking, ownership, and retrieval contract.
- Explain why mock data allows safe product and governance review before sensitive ingestion.

### 2:25–3:20 — Coverage and team views

- Use the coverage matrix to show the breadth of the prototype.
- Highlight that team dashboards are audience entry points over one shared knowledge model, not separate knowledge silos.

### 3:20–4:20 — Q&A retrieval

- Explain query normalization, metadata and chunk search, ranking, confidence, citations, related documents, and next actions.
- Open the working product after this section and ask one representative question if time allows.

### 4:20–5:05 — Ingestion and review

- Clarify that upload and metadata capture are simulated in Version 1.
- Show the intended review-before-indexing flow and the metadata required for governance.

### 5:05–6:10 — Approval and roadmap

- Review the approval checklist.
- Explain the five-phase path from prototype validation to production.

### 6:10–6:40 — Closing

- Summarize what has been validated.
- Ask which authoritative collection should be connected first.

## What to emphasize

- The prototype validates product structure and employee workflows.
- Mock data is deliberately realistic but is not company policy.
- Answers remain traceable to source documents and sections.
- Team dashboards improve entry points while preserving one governed corpus.
- The front-end experience can remain stable while the retrieval and source layers mature.

## What not to claim

- Do not claim the mock guidance is approved or authoritative.
- Do not claim all company systems or documents are connected.
- Do not claim production access controls, storage, monitoring, or AI model synthesis are implemented.
- Do not claim measured time savings or accuracy without a pilot evaluation.
- Do not describe the simulated upload as production ingestion.

## Suggested reviewer questions

- Do the source groups reflect how employees look for knowledge?
- Are team-based entry points useful?
- Is the answer and source format appropriate for internal guidance?
- Which collection should be the first controlled real-data pilot?
- Which documents require restricted access?
- Who should own review, freshness, and retirement?
- What retrieval and citation quality is required for launch?

## Next-step approval plan

1. Approve or revise taxonomy and team dashboards.
2. Select one high-value, low-risk authoritative collection.
3. Assign source owners and permission groups.
4. Define an evaluation question set and expected citations.
5. Run a controlled pilot before expanding ingestion.
