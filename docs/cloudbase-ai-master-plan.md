# CloudBase AI Master Plan

## Product vision

CloudBase AI is a prototype internal company intelligence and automation platform. It organizes company knowledge into a source-backed, role-aware foundation that can later support guided workflows and carefully governed automation.

It assists teams by reducing search effort, improving consistency, and making operational knowledge easier to reuse. It does not replace employee judgment, source ownership, or human review.

## Problem and proposed solution

Useful guidance may span internal sites, SOPs, playbooks, project documents, templates, field references, technical notes, and individual experience. CloudBase AI provides a structured source map, metadata-aware retrieval, team dashboards, planned workflow modules, and a governed document lifecycle.

## Architecture layers

1. **Knowledge Layer** — Ten source groups representing company knowledge.
2. **Intelligence Layer** — Metadata, classification, chunking, ranking, confidence, sources, and next actions.
3. **Team Guidance Layer** — Sales, Field, PM/PL, Technical, Leadership, New Employee, and All Employee views.
4. **Workflow Automation Layer** — Ten planned assistants for answers, intake, handoffs, readiness, QA, ingestion, and onboarding.
5. **Governance Layer** — Ownership, approval, confidentiality, access, versioning, audit, human review, and retirement.

## User groups

- All Employees: source discovery and general process guidance
- Sales: discovery, RFPs, proposal readiness, and delivery handoff
- Field: readiness, mobile collection, documentation, and office handoff
- PM/PL: kickoff, milestones, communication, risk, and delivery
- Technical: automation intake, script review, processing, and QA
- Leadership: coverage, gaps, project health, and automation opportunities
- New Employees: onboarding, learning paths, templates, and role readiness

## Knowledge source structure

Every mock document uses a typed contract: title, source group, document type, department, audience, owner, category, summary, sections, chunks, tags, related documents, example questions, status, confidentiality, and replacement plan.

## Governance model

Real source proposed → owner assigned → metadata and confidentiality captured → human review → permissions applied → approved for indexing → retrieval evaluation → publication → periodic review and retirement.

## Roadmap

1. Prototype foundation
2. Approved SOP pilot
3. COM and team-resource mapping
4. Approved AI and workflow-assistance layer
5. Governance, audit, monitoring, and production deployment
