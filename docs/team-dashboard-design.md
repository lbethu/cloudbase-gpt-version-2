# Team Dashboard Design

## Goal

Team dashboards reduce search effort by presenting the knowledge, checklists, and sample questions most relevant to an employee's role.

## Dashboards

### All Employees

COM navigation, internal resources, onboarding, SOP lookup, and general knowledge-base use.

### Sales Team

Discovery, client pain points, RFP qualification, proposal response, follow-up, and PM/PL handoff.

### Field Team

Field preparation, equipment readiness, mobile collection, field notes, photos, synchronization, and office acceptance.

### PM/PL Team

Kickoff, decision rights, milestone reviews, client updates, risk escalation, QA closure, and delivery readiness.

### Technical Team

Automation requests, Python and ArcGIS review, reproducible processing, automated QA evidence, deployment controls, and knowledge ingestion.

## Design principle

Dashboards are audience filters, not separate knowledge silos. A document can serve multiple audiences and remains one governed source with one owner and review lifecycle.
