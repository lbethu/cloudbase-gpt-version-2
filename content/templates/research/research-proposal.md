## Problem
What is broken, slow, expensive, risky or impossible today? One paragraph, in the words of the person who feels it.

## Why it matters
Consequence of not solving it (cost, quality, risk, missed revenue, client trust).

## Business context
Service line(s), market segment, strategic priority this supports.

## Client context
Named client needs or RFP signals (governed references only — do not paste confidential proposal text).

## Existing internal knowledge
What CloudBase already knows: related SOPs, capabilities, R&D projects, research, project references. Search first; list ids.

## Research question
A single answerable question. "Can X do Y under Z constraints to level W?"

## Hypothesis
What we expect to be true and the observable that would confirm it.

## Technical options
| Option | Approach | Pros | Cons | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Data requirements
Datasets, formats, volume, ownership, licensing, sensitivity.

## External research needed
Papers, vendor documentation, Esri/ArcGIS alignment, industry benchmarks.

## Risks
Technical, data, security, client, schedule.

## Security
Data classification, where processing happens, credentials, third-party services.

## Success criteria
Measurable thresholds that would justify promotion (e.g. R2 → R3).

## Failure criteria
Results that should stop the work. Write these before starting.

## Time / effort assumptions
People, hours, calendar window, tooling cost.

## Recommended next step
Technical spike / experiment plan / tool comparison — and who owns it.
